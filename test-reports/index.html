  • Interview page on phones: a second Save button showed next to the one in the bottom bar.
  • Organiser overview on small phones: the labels of the number cards were cut short (“Candid…”). The icon now sits above the number.
  • Server: the company list is shared by all phones asking at the same moment, and calling a token updates it in place instead of reading every company again.
